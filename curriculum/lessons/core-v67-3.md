## Topic

We don't just learn facts. We run simulations, stress-test beliefs, and practice getting stronger from disorder.

## The Steelman Prompt

What is the strongest argument that simulation practice is just playing and doesn't produce real knowledge?

## Example

Family runs a 10-person Monte Carlo on 'what if the power grid fails for 14 days?' They update their 72-hour plan based on the weakest links the sim revealed.
