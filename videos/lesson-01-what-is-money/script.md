Kid-safe narration for ages about 7–12. Illustrated props only: no real children’s faces or names, and no personal details.

## Institute Bot outline (verbatim)

Script outline (about 75 seconds)

1. **0–8s, hook:** a kid holds up lemonade. "I want stickers… but my friend doesn't want lemonade."
2. **8–25s, the problem:** quick animated chain of trades with someone who has a toy car. "This is called the double coincidence of wants."
3. **25–45s, the fix:** coins appear and the trades become simple. "Money is something everyone agrees to accept."
4. **45–65s, three jobs:** icons for trade 🔄, measuring stick 📏 and saving 🐿️.
5. **65–75s, call to action:** "Try the Swap Market with your family. Lesson 1 at AUBIEETERNAL Institute."

Studio notes: the hook is shown with illustrated lemonade and stickers, not a real child on camera. Later Institute Bot fixes changed the “everyone agrees” line to “lots of people agree,” changed the measuring-stick line to “helps us compare prices,” and asked for “Money fixes this.” to be held on screen for 2–3 seconds. Those fixes are in the beats above and in `lesson-01-final.mp4`.
