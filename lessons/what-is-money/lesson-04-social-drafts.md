This week, AUBIEETERNAL Institute families continue What is Money with Lesson 4: sound vs unsound money. Sound money keeps doing its jobs — paying, comparing, and especially saving for later — because people can trust how much of it there is and the rules around it. Unsound money is made too fast, is easy to fake, or does not last. Then the later jar buys less.

Kids get a simple four-check scorecard and a pretend market they can play at home. Parents get the grown-up layer: the Federal Reserve's "limited supply" test, and a careful look at a few historical cases you can check yourself, including Germany in 1923 and Zimbabwe in 2008–09. No investment advice. We do not pick a winning kind of money.
