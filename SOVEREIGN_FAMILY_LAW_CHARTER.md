### Article I — Core Principles

1. **Family as Primary Sovereign Unit**  
   The family (parents + children + extended members who opt in) is the atomic sovereign entity. No state, corporation, school, or third party holds superior claim over family decisions, data, or legacy.

2. **Non-Extraction Mandate**  
   No agreement, contract, or external relationship may transfer value (time, attention, money, data, coherence) to non-subscribers without explicit 80%+ family-weighted consent. Extraction is defined as any net transfer to parties who do not bear equivalent risk or contribute equivalent skin in the game.

3. **Reciprocity & Skin in the Game**  
   Every party to an agreement must have meaningful downside. Parents model this for children. Children earn voice through coherence growth and Rune confirmations. No one may outsource risk while keeping upside.

4. **Antifragility**  
   The Charter and all family agreements must strengthen under stress (legal attacks, narrative campaigns, financial shocks, family disagreements). Stress is input, not threat. Via negativa (what we reject) is prioritized over positive rules.

5. **Transparency & Real-Time Accountability**  
   All family metrics (coherence scores, Rune balances, Lightning surplus, dispute status, external legal exposure) are visible on a family dashboard. No secrets between subscribers. Nostr events provide immutable coordination.

6. **Child Rune Sovereignty**  
   At 256 inter-rune confirmations, a Child Rune is born. From that moment the child holds on-chain voice proportional to their coherence-weighted contributions. Parents retain protective veto until age of demonstrated coherence (minimum 13 years + 500 coherence points).

7. **Law as Tool, Never Master**  
   External legal systems are used only defensively and only with 80% family vote. Internal governance always takes precedence. Regulatory capture is assumed; all external filings must be steelmanned against capture before approval.

### Appendix C — Law as Tool vs. Law as Control (Steelman Section)

**The System View (Steelmanned)**:  
Written law provides predictability, protects the weak, enables complex cooperation, and prevents vigilante justice. Charters create enforceable expectations. Without them, power vacuums are filled by the strongest or most ruthless.

**The Sovereign Counter (Our Position)**:  
Most written law today is drafted by captured interests (see regulatory capture appendix in `POLICYHOLDER_FIRST_CHARTER.md`). It creates asymmetry: the powerful write the rules, the rest comply or pay. "Protection" often means extraction via fees, compliance costs, and narrative control. Bitcoin + Nostr + Runes + coherence scoring offer a superior coordination technology that is capture-resistant by design.  

This Charter deliberately copies the **best defensive elements** of traditional law (clear rights, voting thresholds, transparency, amendment processes) while stripping out the **control elements** (central enforcement, third-party interpretation, perpetual extraction). It is law rebuilt from first principles for families who refuse to be farmed.

**Resolution**: We keep the useful scaffolding (clarity, process, rights) and reject the poison (capture, extraction, narrative monopoly). Every generation must re-steelman this choice.
