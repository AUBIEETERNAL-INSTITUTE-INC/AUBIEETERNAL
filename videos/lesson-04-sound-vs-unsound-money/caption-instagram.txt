Maya's lemonade costs 1 pretend note. Then the note keeper makes 10 more notes. Same lemonade — now it costs 2. 🍋

That's the heart of sound vs unsound money. Sound money keeps doing its jobs, especially saving for later, because people can trust the supply and the rules. If too much money is made too fast, the later jar buys less.
