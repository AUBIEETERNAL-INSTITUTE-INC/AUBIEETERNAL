🍋 Lesson 4: sound vs unsound money

- Sound money keeps doing its jobs — especially saving for later — because people can trust the supply and the rules.
- If too many pretend notes are made, the same later jar buys less.
- Try the Sound or Unsound game at home with a four-check scorecard. Pretend only.
- For parents: limited supply, trust, and a few history cases you can check (Germany 1923, Zimbabwe 2008–09). No investment advice.
