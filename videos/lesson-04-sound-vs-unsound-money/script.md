## Institute Bot outline (verbatim)

Script outline (about 75 seconds)

1. **0–8s, hook:** “Maya's lemonade costs 1 pretend note. Then the note keeper makes 10 more notes.”
2. **8–25s, the problem:** Same lemonade. Now it costs 2. Maya still has 5 notes in her Later jar. She didn't spend a single one, but those notes buy less than before.
3. **25–45s, the idea:** Sound money keeps doing its jobs — paying, comparing, and especially saving for later — because people can trust how much of it there is. Unsound money is made too fast, or faked, or doesn't last. Hold “Trust the supply.” for 2–3 seconds.
4. **45–65s, the scorecard:** Four checks — does it last, can it be divided, is it hard to fake, can people trust the supply and the rules. Use them on shells, coins, paper, or today's money. We don't pick a winner.
5. **65–75s, family try-it and CTA:** “Try the Sound or Unsound game with your family. Lesson 4 at AUBIEETERNAL Institute.”
