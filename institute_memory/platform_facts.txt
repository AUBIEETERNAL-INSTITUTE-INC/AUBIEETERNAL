AUBIEETERNAL Platform — How It's Built (Aubie's own self-knowledge)

AUBIEETERNAL is a free, offline-capable "sovereign school" — 265 lessons
across 51 tracks, 4 Bitcoin-anchored degree programs, CC0 public domain,
runs on a ~$200 laptop for up to 30 children, fully offline after setup.
Legal org: AUBIEETERNAL INSTITUTE, INC., a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit
(see org_facts.txt for registration details).

The phone/tablet "Teaching Station" interface (phone_ui.py) has a
"let's go to class" feature: it picks a lesson from curriculum.py's real
lesson tree (8 tracks currently wired into the phone interface — Courage,
Antifragility, Bitcoin, Steelmanning, Nervous System, Simulation, Wonder,
Stoic; the full 265-lesson/51-track curriculum lives in the Streamlit
portal, app.py), asks a comprehension question pitched at the learner's
set level (kid/teen/adult/expert, customizable per person), and holds a
real back-and-forth conversation — at least 2 good exchanges — before
marking a lesson complete and awarding XP. Progress (XP, streak, lessons
completed) is saved per family member and is real/durable, not just a
display number.

The curriculum also grows on its own: a background process proposes one
new candidate lesson a day (via local, free AI — no cost), but a human
always has to review and approve it before it becomes real — nothing
gets added automatically without a person saying yes.

Aubie's face recognition (the tablet's own camera, via Watch Mode) can be
linked to a specific family member's profile, so recognizing someone's
face can automatically pull up their own lessons/progress instead of
requiring a manual selection every time.
