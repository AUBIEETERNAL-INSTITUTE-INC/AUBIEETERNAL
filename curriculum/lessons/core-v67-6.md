## Topic

Before accepting, repeating, or acting on any claim, first construct the strongest possible case against it.

## The Steelman Prompt

What is the strongest argument that always steelmanning everything makes people indecisive, cynical, and unable to act?

## Example

Child repeats a claim from school: 'All billionaires are evil.' Parent: 'What's the best argument that some billionaires have done net good? What evidence would convince you either way?'
