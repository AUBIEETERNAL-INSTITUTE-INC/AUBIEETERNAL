AUBIEETERNAL Sovereign University exists to deliver university-level education to every family on earth, without tuition, paperwork, gatekeepers, or institutional capture.

It was built as a sovereign lattice: each family maintains full control over their data, curriculum choices, and pace, while participating in a shared, high-coherence knowledge network secured by Bitcoin Runes, Nostr, and the Epistemic Commons.

## Article I — Mission

**1. Epistemic Sovereignty for Families**
Every family that completes the curriculum will have the tools to evaluate evidence, resist manipulation, understand complex systems, and reason honestly under uncertainty.

**2. Humanitarian Deployment**
Every PhD graduate must deploy sovereign infrastructure for a community that lacks it. The university grows by serving, not by credentialing.

**3. Public Epistemic Signal**
Every steelman, research experiment, calibrated belief, and honest assessment logged by AUBIEETERNAL families is published to the Epistemic Commons as CC0. Individual learning becomes civilizational infrastructure.

## Article II — Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

### 2.1 Epistemic Rigor First
Every lesson, quest, and proposal must be steelmanned, simulation-tested, and coherence-scored before approval. Rigor means clarity, evidence, and the ability to defend or improve an idea under scrutiny.

### 2.2 Family Sovereignty and Local-First
All data, progress, and decision-making remain under family control. The system defaults to local inference (Ollama). Families can run the entire university offline. Fork independence is protected.

### 2.3 Antifragility and Simulation as Practice
Learning must increase the learner's ability to gain from disorder. Every track includes simulation, hypothesis testing, and real-world application.

### 2.4 Non-Extraction
The university shall never monetize family data, charge hidden fees, or create dependency on paid services. All rewards are transparent and earned through genuine learning. 100% of earned sats remain under family control.

### 2.5 On-Chain Identity via Child Runes
Progress is anchored on Bitcoin through Child Rune fragments. 256 fragments = Child Rune Genesis ceremony. This record belongs to the child, not any institution.

## Article III — Academic Standards

### The Core Standard
A graduate of AUBIEETERNAL must be able to:
- Identify the strongest argument against their own position (steelmanning)
- Evaluate primary evidence, not just expert summaries
- Acknowledge uncertainty with calibrated confidence
- Update beliefs when evidence demands it
- Apply Monte Carlo stress-testing to their most important arguments
- Recognize all 20 epistemic attack vectors in the Truth Frequency Analyzer

### No Grade Inflation
Degrees are awarded when demonstrated mastery is verified — not when time is served. The coherence threshold is enforced by the system.

### Capstone Non-Negotiability
The PhD capstone requires:
- A working piece of infrastructure with documented adoption by ≥3 external families
- A CC0 contribution merged into the AUBIEETERNAL repo
- Child Rune Genesis (256 Bitcoin confirmations)
- An honest 500-word post-mortem including what failed

There is no pathway to the PhD that avoids these requirements.

## Article V — Governance Model

### Three-Layer Structure

**Family Layer:** Full veto rights over any lesson or track. Right to opt out without penalty.

**Operator Layer:** Primary operator (@hodlmateo) maintains core codebase, approves high-impact changes, ensures coherence with charter.

**Swarm Layer:** Evolution engine proposes lessons, scores coherence, surfaces patterns. Final approval requires operator sign-off and swarm coherence ≥ 0.85.

### Amendment Process
1. Proposal submitted (marked "Charter Amendment")
2. Steelman round (3+ arguments for and against)
3. Swarm coherence evaluation (minimum 0.88)
4. 30-day community comment period on LatticeFeed
5. Operator approval + 15% family veto threshold

*Emergency patches* allowed for critical issues; must be reviewed within 14 days or expire automatically.

## Article VI — Curriculum Governance

### Who Can Add Courses
Anyone. The curriculum is CC0. Submit a pull request. Evaluated on:
- Epistemic depth
- Self-upgradeability (works at age 5 AND PhD depth)
- Falsifiability
- PhD extension (genuine research-level challenge)
- Local inference compatibility (runs on standard Ollama)

### Submission and Review Process
1. Automatic intake → logged in `curriculum-proposals/`
2. Community comment period (48–72 hours on LatticeFeed)
3. Swarm coherence scoring (below 0.70 = auto-rejected with feedback)
4. Operator review with steelmanning
5. Publication to Curriculum Map and `family_hud.py`

Rejected proposals receive written rationale. One appeal allowed. Second rejection is final.

### What Gets Removed
Lessons that misrepresent evidence. No ideology. No political advocacy. The steelman requirement applies to all curriculum content.

### Versioning
Every lesson change is committed to GitHub. Every major version is Bitcoin-anchored. Families can always verify they are running the same curriculum their ancestors ran.
