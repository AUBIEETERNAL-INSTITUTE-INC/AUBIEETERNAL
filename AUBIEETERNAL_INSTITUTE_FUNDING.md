## Institutional to-dos (not grants, but related)

- **`.edu` domain — flagged, needs a reality check.** `.edu` is restricted by EDUCAUSE (the registry operator) to institutions **accredited by a U.S. Department of Education-recognized accrediting agency** — it is not available just for being an education-focused nonprofit or a registered 501(c)(3). AUBIEETERNAL would need to pursue actual institutional accreditation (a real, multi-year process, separate from nonprofit status) before `.edu` is possible. Until/unless that's pursued, a `.org` domain is the standard, immediately-available choice for a nonprofit. Worth a dedicated research pass if formal accreditation is a real goal — different question from grant funding.
