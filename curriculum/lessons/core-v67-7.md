## Topic

An idea is only as good as its ability to survive being stress-tested in mental, computational, and real-world simulations.

## The Steelman Prompt

Simulations are just stories we tell ourselves; they can't predict black swans or real chaos.

## Example

The family runs a full Monte Carlo simulation of 'both parents lose income for two years while inflation is 12%.' They discover three hidden single points of failure and fix them before they happen.
