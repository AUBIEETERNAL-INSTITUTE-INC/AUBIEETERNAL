## How It Works

### Dual HUD System

| Role     | What They See                                      | Purpose |
|----------|----------------------------------------------------|--------|
| **Kid**  | Age-appropriate lesson + steelmanning prompt + coherence meter | Focused learning experience |
| **Parent** | Live view of child’s coherence, polyvagal state, progress, and stuck points | Real-time guidance and co-regulation |

The parent becomes a **co-learner and guide**, not just a supervisor.

### Session Flow

1. Child starts a lesson (e.g., “Courage — Level 2”)
2. Both see the lesson and steelmanning prompt
3. Child gives their answer out loud (or types it)
4. System scores the answer and updates coherence in real time
5. Parent can encourage, pause, or join the session at any moment
6. At the end of the session, XP is awarded and progress is saved to the swarm
