## Topic

The Core is not a finished product. Each family that masters it has a duty to improve it and pass a stronger version to the next generation and to other families.

## The Steelman Prompt

Each generation should tear everything down and start over instead of inheriting 'truths' from flawed ancestors.

## Example

After completing all 10 Core v67 lessons, the family writes one new lesson or improvement based on what they learned in their real life. They commit it to their private fork and send the diff to two other families running the Core.
