# Grokipedia growth (local)

- `grokipedia_principles.json` — approved entries (also mirrored under `/mnt/main/`).
- `grokipedia_sources.txt` — topics to draft from (edit freely).
- `grokipedia_grow.py` — nightly drafts via local `qwen2.5:14b`, heat-guarded.
- `grokipedia_approve.py` — `list` / `add` / `reject` / `skip` for Discord & SimpleX.

Pending drafts live in `/mnt/main/grokipedia_pending.json` until you approve them.
Nothing is added to the encyclopedia without a human Add.
