What We Teach

Three tracks. One mission:
you own what you build.

Every program is hands-on, curriculum-linked, and designed so students walk away understanding — not just using — the technology in front of them.

Computer Building

UnoQ AI Tutor

Students build and operate a personalized AI tutor node — touch display, webcam, voice, and local LLM — running entirely on their own hardware. No cloud. No subscription. The AI works for them, not the other way around.

About the Institute

Built on the belief that
technology should be understood, not just used.

AUBIEETERNAL Institute is a Florida-based nonprofit founded to give families and students real, sovereign technology education. We focus on epistemic rigor — teaching not just how to use AI tools, but how they work, who controls them, and how to build alternatives you own.

Our free, open-source curriculum and software is available for home or institutional use. Everything we build is designed to be understood, modified, and owned by the people using it.

For Families New to This

What is homeschooling in Florida?
And is this legit?

Homeschooling in Florida is a fully legal, state-recognized option for families. You don't need to be a credentialed teacher. Florida law gives parents the right to direct their children's education at home — and it's been this way for decades. AUBIEETERNAL Institute offers curriculum and hands-on programs that homeschool families can use as part of their portfolio of education.
